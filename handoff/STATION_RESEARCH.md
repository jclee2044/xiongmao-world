# Suzhou station research and design boundaries

Verified on October 10, 2026 for the station exterior build in Xiongmao World. The proposed journey is credible as a compressed Suzhou-inspired game level. The illustrations are not a verified reproduction of Suzhou Station's geography, floor plan, road layout or operating timetable.

## Boat arrival and the station forecourt

Suzhou's city-management department documented a water-bus route between the railway station's south dock and Shantang Street's north dock in October 2023. Its report also describes a road beside the river and changes to vehicle and bicycle circulation. This directly supports combining a boat arrival, pedestrian approach and nearby road. It does not establish the bridge or road positions in our illustration. [City-management report](https://cgj.suzhou.gov.cn/srsz/qxdt/202310/1a20c02defeb440ebffeb9432e32baed.shtml)

The city's March 2025 travel guide identifies the south-square ground-floor entrance as suitable for water-bus passengers and describes a sightseeing route from the station dock via Shantang Street. A June 2025 report confirms further services from the south-square dock. These establish the connection, not a guaranteed current schedule, fare, vessel design or exact reverse journey. Do not reproduce old fares or intervals as current travel advice. [March guide](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/dstx/202503/5ad529df6c3149468ff915cf5ec07bb4.shtml), [June report](https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/szyw/202506/25a525bbdc924f95b43f27c3500fee49.shtml)

The bridge in the concept is an authored game connection. The sources do not verify that passengers must cross that kind of bridge after landing at the real station dock. Keep the user-requested bridge, but describe the district as an illustrated interpretation. Do not name this invented bridge after a real one.

The road at the right edge should be a fictional peripheral access road. A May 2026 transport-department report discusses further changes to south-square pickup/drop-off organization; it does not publish a finished arrangement matching the drawing. Avoid presenting the pictured taxi canopy as the real taxi rank. [Transport-department report](https://jtj.suzhou.gov.cn/szjt/yzxx/202605/fc00dd9e29f34f298af00d675de3ec46.shtml)

## Architecture and scale

The station architect describes a large modern transport building incorporating white walls, dark tiles, gardens, corridors and rhombus motifs in roof, window and paving designs. This supports the regional character and substantial scale. The concept's wood, flowers, lantern-like lights, tiny canal and intimate frontage are artistic treatments, not a measured representation. Keep modern structural bays, generous glazing and a building extending beyond the playable area. [China Architecture Design and Research Group](https://cadg.com.cn/en/works/552/)

## Ticketing and security

The useful game sequence is: arrive at the public forecourt and ticket office; buy a Shanghai-bound ticket if not already booked; complete entry identity/ticket verification and security; wait; pass the assigned boarding gate when boarding opens; reach the platform and board.

China Railway supports counter purchases and passport-based ticketing. With an e-ticket, the booking identity document is used for checks; an itinerary printout is not itself a boarding ticket. Buying at a counter is the game's conversation scenario, not something every real passenger must do. Keep ticketing on the public side of the controlled entrance. The illustration's door should open into a public ticketing vestibule, with the actual passenger-entry checkpoint beyond. The precise order and placement of entry verification and screening can vary; this game should not teach its arrangement as universal. [12306 passenger FAQ](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=2)

12306 describes staffed channels for documents not handled by its listed automatic readers. A staffed passport check is a suitable interaction; do not claim every passport works at every automatic gate, or that passports universally cannot be read. [12306 identity-document guidance](https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/gonggao/realNameTicket.html)

The railway security regulation, effective February 2024, specifies screening equipment and staff. Article 25 explicitly requires separation between screened and unscreened areas; someone who leaves the screened area must be screened again on return. Thus the original open bypass was inaccurate. The continuous partition and staffed detector/conveyor lanes fix the principle, but their exact glass construction is our design. Passengers walk through the detector; bags travel through the scanner and are collected beyond it. Boarding gates perform a later check and should not be depicted as the main metal detectors. [National Railway Administration, especially Articles 8, 18 and 25](https://source.nra.gov.cn/xxgk/gkml/ztjg/gfzd/bmgz/202401/t20240102_344299.shtml)

## Train levels and boarding time

For this Suzhou-inspired setting, use an above-ground intercity railway platform, not an underground metro platform. A secondary station-layout guide distinguishes first-floor railway platforms, a second-floor waiting hall, underground arrivals and deeper metro platforms. It also lists a first-floor waiting area, so a blanket statement that every passenger must descend from upstairs would be too strong. Exact gate assignments for our fictional service remain unverified. [Station-layout guide, Station Plan section](https://www.travelchinaguide.com/china-trains/suzhou-station.htm)

The city's official guide independently confirms both ground-floor and upper-floor entrances, but does not prove a particular waiting-to-platform route. The earlier architectural-planning link used in chat could not be reopened successfully during this verification; do not rely on it as the sole support for exact elevations.

The subsequent design discussion replaced the earlier flat layout with an upper waiting hall: ascend after ground-floor security, pass the upstairs boarding gates, then descend to the above-ground platform. Provide a lift alternative at both connections. This resolves the concept's ambiguous elevations without claiming to reproduce an exact real boarding route. Interior construction remains separate from the exterior build. An upper hall cannot be connected to a ground-level train by a flat doorway merely because both appear adjacent in an overview.

Real boarding is tied to the booked train and a limited check-in period. Train arrival is not the sole trigger for passengers to enter the platform. The desired short exploration wait and ability to board at the player's own pace are cozy-game accommodations; do not teach indefinite boarding as real practice. [12306 passenger FAQ, pre-departure guidance](https://www.12306.cn/en/faq.html?item=2)
