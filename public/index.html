Your first little adventure

There’s no rush. Explore the town, meet someone new, and try a little Chinese.

  • On your phone: hold and drag to walk. Release to stop, then tap a nearby person or object to interact.
  • On a computer: click where you want to go, or walk with WASD / arrow keys. Press Space near someone to talk.
  • Discover words: tap Chinese words in conversations for their meaning and pinyin. Replay speech when you need another listen.
  • Ask Panda: open Panda’s help for hints, translations, and a nudge toward your next adventure.

Your journal keeps your progress. Voice and online conversations need a connection; offline exploration becomes available after the world finishes downloading.
